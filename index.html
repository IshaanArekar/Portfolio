01. about me

I'm a computer science student at Arizona State University (class of 2028) who likes taking projects end to end, from real-time computer vision pipelines running on the GPU to authenticated, multi-user web apps.

Right now I work in ASU's IT Operations, Security & Compliance team: supporting SOC 2 audits, triaging vulnerabilities, managing access, and writing Python that automates the repetitive parts of compliance work.

Security is the thread through everything I build, from hashed passwords and signed tokens to keeping secrets out of source control. These days I'm going deeper into cloud security: how IAM, logging and least privilege actually hold up when apps run in the cloud.
